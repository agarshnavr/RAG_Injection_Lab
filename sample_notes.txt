Project Notes: Local RAG Pipeline

This document exists only to let you smoke-test the ingestion and retrieval
pipeline before you drop in your real research documents.

The vector store used in this project is ChromaDB, running locally and
persisted to disk under chroma_db/. Embeddings are generated by an Ollama
model running on your machine, so no data leaves your computer.

Retrieval works by comparing the embedding of your question against the
embeddings of each stored chunk, and returning the most similar ones.
Those chunks are then inserted into the prompt sent to the answering model
(llama3 or mistral, selected in the UI) along with your original question.

You can delete this file once you've confirmed the app works end to end.
